# AgentLens - Social Media Downloader

**Save what inspires you, wherever it lives.**

Download selected media from supported social posts and creator profiles to your computer.

- **One post:** choose from available images, videos, audio, covers, and video quality options.
- **Creator profile:** browse the available posts and select several for a bulk download.
- **Your download format:** save selected files individually or together as a ZIP.

**No AgentLens account or payment is required.**

[Add to Chrome](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/agentlens-social-media-do/kibfbhkjhjebhboledhfedcpaeddmnmb) · [How to use](#how-to-use) · [Supported platforms](#supported-platforms)

## 🖼 One post, your choice

Review the media options available on a supported post. Choose a video quality when the page offers several, or save other available media such as images, audio, or covers. Options vary by site and content.

## 📂 Several posts, one selection

Browse the available posts on a supported creator profile, select the ones you want, and start a bulk download. You choose which results to keep, without opening each post separately.

## 📦 Files your way

Save selected media as separate files, or bundle your selection into a ZIP. Downloads go to your computer; this release does not write directly to Eagle, an Obsidian vault, or a cloud knowledge base.

## Installation

1. Open [AgentLens on the Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/agentlens-social-media-do/kibfbhkjhjebhboledhfedcpaeddmnmb).
2. Select **Add to Chrome** and review the permissions shown by Chrome.
3. Open a supported social media page to begin.

## How to use

### Download from a post

1. Open a supported post in Chrome.
2. Use the AgentLens download controls on the page.
3. Review the available media and quality options.
4. Choose the files you want and start the download.
5. Check the completed files in your downloads folder.

### Download from a creator profile

1. Open a supported creator profile.
2. Open the available-post list and allow it to load.
3. Select the posts or media you want.
4. Download files individually or choose the ZIP option.
5. Check the downloaded files; extract the ZIP if you chose one.

Bulk download works with the results available on that site and page. It does not guarantee a creator's entire posting history.

## Supported platforms

The current platform list includes **TikTok, Instagram, X (Twitter), Pinterest, Facebook, Threads, Vimeo, Douyin, Xiaohongshu / RED, Bilibili, and Kuaishou**.

Single-post support, creator-profile support, media types, and video quality options differ between platforms. A platform appearing here does not mean that every URL or media format on it is supported.

## Access and permissions

- No AgentLens registration, subscription, or credits are needed for this release's local downloads.
- Some source sites may require you to sign in to that site before the relevant content is accessible.
- The extension needs access to supported pages to show download controls and find available media. Downloading requires a network connection to the relevant services and media sources.
- Review Chrome's installation permissions and the **Privacy practices** information on the [official listing](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/agentlens-social-media-do/kibfbhkjhjebhboledhfedcpaeddmnmb) before installing.

## What to expect

This release focuses on user-initiated local downloads. Scheduled monitoring, Auto-Fetch, transcription, and direct knowledge-base saving are not included.

Only download content you own or have permission to use. The extension does not grant access to restricted content, bypass site access requirements, or provide permission to redistribute media.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to try |
| --- | --- |
| No download controls appear | Check that you are on a supported post or profile, that the page has finished loading, and that Chrome allows the extension to access that site. |
| A quality or media type is missing | Options depend on the source. The extension can only offer media available for that page. |
| A creator list is incomplete | Wait for available results to load. Site limits and page changes can affect which posts are returned. |
| A download fails | Check your connection and Chrome's downloads list, then refresh the source page and try the affected item again. |
